## Problem Section

**Section Title:**  
*The IPFS Landscape is Broken.*

**Problem 1: Vendor Lock-In**
Pinata, Infura, Web3.Storage. They all look the same, cost the same, and trap you in walled gardens. Your data deserves better.

**Problem 2: No Persistence Guarantees**
How do you know your content is *actually* persisted? Pinning APIs don't prove anything. You're trusting black boxes.

**Problem 3: Hidden Economics**
Storage pricing is opaque. You don't know what you're paying for. You can't predict costs. You can't optimize.

**Problem 4: No Native Encryption**
Most IPFS platforms store your data plaintext. Encryption is an afterthought, added by the client. That's not good enough for enterprises.

**Problem 5: Isolated from Blockchain**
Your smart contracts can't trigger IPFS actions. Your DAOs can't automate storage. Your dApps are disconnected from persistence.

Content automatically moves to Filecoin. Deals are recorded on-chain. Proofs are verified publicly. Persistence you can trust.
